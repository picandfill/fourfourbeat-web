One, two, tap!

Tap the moment the red ball lands on the color word after "one, two". 75 minigames switch nonstop in a rhythm game like no other.

Download on the App Store Google Play coming soon

Tap · Hold · Flick

Tap on the beat, hold and release, flick the way the arrow points. Colors tell you on-beat, double and off-beat taps, so you get it from the first try.

Three modes

Endless keeps speeding up (up to 300 BPM), Perfect runs all 75 games at 220 BPM, and Hardcore gives you no hearts back.

Fever mode

Clear minigames in a row without a MISS and the fever climbs step by step. The tempo rises, the screen heats up, and your score goes up to 6×!

Compete worldwide

This device, national and world rankings for each mode and overall. No sign-in needed.

Dress up Popo

Earn notes from daily check-ins, today's missions and play, then change Popo's color, shape and background in the shop.

Badges & rhythm age

Collect 34 badges, and see your rhythm age from your score after every run.
