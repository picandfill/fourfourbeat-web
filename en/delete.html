Delete your account and data

FourFour Beat (developer: Taegabi Studio) joins the rankings with an anonymous account, with no login. You can delete your ranking records and anonymous account on the server as follows.

Delete in the app

  1. Open FourFour Beat and tap Settings on the main menu.
  2. Tap Delete Data.
  3. Tap Delete to confirm.

Your ranking records on the server (nickname, country, scores and so on) and your anonymous account are deleted right away, along with the records, badges, settings, notes and items saved on your phone. If you are offline, the server copy is deleted the next time you open the app.

If you already deleted the app

Email contact@taegabi.com with "Data deletion request". Since there is no login, we cannot find your account by email, so please include the nickname, country and approximate best score you used on the rankings.

We delete those records and the anonymous account within 7 days and reply to let you know.

What is deleted and what remains

  • Deleted: the anonymous user ID, and all modes' records and overall score (nickname, country, best score, minigames cleared, PERFECT/GOOD/MISS counts, time of the record)
  • Nothing remains: we do not keep deleted records. Only the deletion request email is deleted within one year after we reply.
