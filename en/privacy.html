Privacy Policy

This is a translation of the Korean policy. If they differ, the Korean version applies.

Taegabi Studio ("we") protects the personal information of people who use the FourFour Beat app ("the app") under Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and related laws, and keeps this policy so that concerns can be handled quickly. The app is a rhythm game with no sign-up or login, and it does not collect information that directly identifies you, such as your name, email address or phone number.

You can read this policy at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Policy and on this website.

1. Information we process and why

Kept only on your phone: nickname, country, settings (volume, vibration, sync offset), this-device rankings, badges, play stats, notes, check-ins, today's mission progress and the items you bought. It is deleted when you delete the app. Of these, only your nickname and country are also stored with the ranking record below.

For the national and world rankings, the following is stored on our server when you set a new best score.

IdentifierA randomly generated anonymous user ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication). It is not linked to a name or email addressTelling your records apart, letting only you change your records
Ranking recordFor each mode (Endless, Perfect, Hardcore): nickname, country, best score, minigames cleared, PERFECT/GOOD/MISS counts, time of the record, and the total of the three modes' best scores (overall)Showing national and world rankings, filtering out impossible scores

ReportsThe anonymous IDs of the reporter and the reported player, the reported nickname, the time of the reportReviewing and removing inappropriate nicknames

The nickname, country and score on the rankings are visible to other players. Do not put your real name, contact details or anything else that identifies you in your nickname. If you do not choose a country, nothing is sent to the server.

Ads: to keep the app free, it shows banner ads at the top and bottom of the screen, and a rewarded ad when you choose "Watch an ad to continue" after a game over. The ads are served by Google AdMob, for which Google processes your device's advertising identifier (Android advertising ID, iOS IDFA), approximate location (based on IP address), device and app information, and records of ads seen and tapped. On iOS, the IDFA is used only if you choose "Allow tracking"; the app works the same if you don't (you then see non-personalized ads). We do not receive or store advertising identifiers ourselves.

2. How long we keep it, and deletion

  • Ranking records and anonymous IDs on the server: until you delete them. Settings → Delete Data in the app deletes them immediately. If you are offline at that moment, they are deleted the next time you open the app.
  • Information on your phone: deleted by Settings → Delete Data or by deleting the app.
  • Report records: deleted within one year after they are handled.
  • Block list: kept only on your phone; deleted by Settings → Delete Data or by deleting the app.
  • Inquiry emails: deleted within one year after we reply.
  • Where another law requires us to keep something, we keep it separately for that period.

How we delete: electronic files are erased so they cannot be recovered. We do not print or keep personal information on paper.

3. Sharing with third parties

We do not give your personal information to other companies or people. However, the nickname, country and score on the rankings are visible to other players, and we may provide information when an investigative authority requests it through the procedure set by law. If you agree to personalized ads, Google may also use the ad information in section 4 to improve its own ad services (How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services).

If you do not want this transfer, do not choose a country, or use Settings → Delete Data; you can still play with the this-device rankings. Ad information is transferred over the internet (encrypted connection) when ads are loaded and shown, and Google keeps it as described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can turn off personalized ads as described in section 6.

5. Phone permissions

Apart from internet access and the advertising identifier (Android advertising ID; on iOS, the "Allow tracking" prompt), the app does not ask for permissions such as photos, location, contacts or microphone.

6. Automatic collection (cookies)

The app itself does not use cookies, but Google AdMob uses the advertising identifier for ads. If you do not want personalized ads, on Android go to Settings → Google → Ads and delete the advertising ID; on iOS go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and turn off tracking for the app. Depending on your region you can also change this in the app under Settings → Ad Privacy Settings. This website uses no visitor analytics; Google may process connection information (such as IP address) when fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

7. Your rights and those of legal guardians

You (or your legal guardian if you are under 14) can ask at any time to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information.

  • Change nickname or country: right away in the app's Profile; the rankings update too.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Data in the app.
  • If you already deleted the app: see Delete Account and email contact@taegabi.com. We delete within 7 days and let you know.
  • Other requests: email us at the address below and we will handle it without delay.

8. Children under 14

The app does not collect information that directly identifies a person, such as a name or contact details. A legal guardian of a child under 14 can ask us at the address below to see or delete the child's records.

9. Security measures

  • All connections between the app and the server are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Server access rules let players write and delete only their own records and only read others'. The server rejects impossible scores and records sent too often.
  • Only we hold server admin rights, kept to the minimum needed.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we announce it in the app or on this page 7 days before it takes effect (30 days if the change is unfavorable to you).
